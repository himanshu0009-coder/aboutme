## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hello! I'm a **Data Analyst** passionate about transforming raw data into meaningful insights.

I enjoy working with data to uncover **patterns, trends, opportunities, and business insights** that can support better decision-making.

My focus is on combining **technical analysis with business thinking** to answer important questions such as:

> 💡 What happened?
> 🔎 Why did it happen?
> 📈 What can we learn from the data?
> 🎯 What should we do next?
